# DesertTech Solutions – Privacy Risk Assessment

## Purpose

This fictional assessment identifies privacy risks associated with DesertTech Solutions' processing of personal data.

The assessment follows:

**Risk → Control → Evidence**

---

## Risk 1 – Excessive Access to Customer Data

**Risk:** Customer personal data stored without appropriate access restrictions.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer names and email addresses.

**Threat:** Unauthorized employee access.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Role-based access and quarterly access reviews.

**Recommended Treatment:** Strengthen least-privilege controls and perform quarterly access reviews.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

## Risk 2 – Employee Payroll Exposure

**Risk:** Unauthorized access to employee payroll information.

**Personal Data Involved:** Employee salary and payroll information.

**Threat:** Insider misuse or compromised account.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Restricted HR access and MFA.

**Recommended Treatment:** Implement stronger privileged access monitoring and periodic HR access reviews.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

## Risk 3 – Passport Copy Exposure

**Risk:** Unauthorized access to employee passport copies.

**Personal Data Involved:** Passport and identity information.

**Threat:** Unauthorized access or data leakage.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Restricted document storage.

**Recommended Treatment:** Encrypt sensitive identity documents and restrict access using least privilege.

**Residual Risk:** Medium

---

## Risk 4 – Excessive Data Retention

**Risk:** Personal data retained longer than necessary.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer and former employee information.

**Threat:** Unnecessary data exposure.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** Medium

**Existing Control:** Basic retention practices.

**Recommended Treatment:** Establish formal retention schedules and automated deletion where appropriate.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

## Risk 5 – Third-Party Processor Breach

**Risk:** A supplier processing DesertTech personal data experiences a security incident.

**Personal Data Involved:** Employee personal information.

**Threat:** Third-party compromise.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Vendor risk assessment and contractual security requirements.

**Recommended Treatment:** Conduct annual assessments of high-risk processors and monitor security evidence.

**Residual Risk:** Medium

---

## Risk 6 – Cross-Border Data Transfer

**Risk:** Personal data is transferred or stored outside the UAE without appropriate assessment.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer personal information.

**Threat:** Unmanaged international processing.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Basic vendor review.

**Recommended Treatment:** Maintain a cross-border transfer inventory and assess applicable legal requirements and safeguards.

**Residual Risk:** Medium

---

## Risk 7 – Weak Data Deletion

**Risk:** Personal data remains in systems after the applicable retention period.

**Personal Data Involved:** Former employee and customer data.

**Threat:** Unauthorized access to unnecessary data.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** Medium

**Existing Control:** Manual deletion processes.

**Recommended Treatment:** Establish documented deletion procedures and retain deletion evidence.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

## Risk 8 – Privacy Incident Not Escalated

**Risk:** A cybersecurity incident involving personal data is not identified or escalated as a privacy incident.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer or employee personal data.

**Threat:** Delayed incident assessment and response.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** High

**Existing Control:** Incident Response Plan.

**Recommended Treatment:** Add privacy escalation criteria and conduct incident response exercises involving GRC/privacy stakeholders.

**Residual Risk:** Medium

---

## Risk 9 – Unclear Processing Purpose

**Risk:** Personal data is processed for purposes that are not clearly documented.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer information.

**Threat:** Uncontrolled or inappropriate processing.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** Medium

**Existing Control:** Basic business process documentation.

**Recommended Treatment:** Create a processing activity register documenting purpose, data categories, recipients, retention, and applicable legal basis.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

## Risk 10 – Data Subject Request Not Tracked

**Risk:** A valid data-subject request is not handled consistently or within applicable requirements.

**Personal Data Involved:** Customer or employee personal data.

**Threat:** Manual processing failure.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Impact:** Medium

**Existing Control:** General customer service process.

**Recommended Treatment:** Create a formal data-subject request workflow with ownership, verification, tracking, and evidence.

**Residual Risk:** Low

---

# Risk → Control → Evidence

## Example 1

### Risk
Unauthorized access to customer personal data.

### Control
Role-based access control and quarterly access reviews.

### Evidence

- Access control policy
- User access review
- Access approval records
- MFA report

---

## Example 2

### Risk
Personal data retained longer than necessary.

### Control
Data retention and secure deletion process.

### Evidence

- Data retention schedule
- Deletion procedure
- Deletion logs
- System configuration

---

## Example 3

### Risk
Third-party processor exposes personal data.

### Control
Vendor risk assessment and contractual security requirements.

### Evidence

- Vendor assessment
- Security questionnaire
- Contract
- ISO 27001 / SOC 2 evidence where applicable
- Vendor review records

---

# GRC Analyst Perspective

A privacy risk assessment should not stop at identifying a risk.

A GRC Analyst should connect:

**Privacy Risk**
↓
**Security / Privacy Control**
↓
**Control Owner**
↓
**Evidence**
↓
**Testing**
↓
**Finding**
↓
**Corrective Action**
↓
**Residual Risk**

This creates a defensible privacy governance process.
